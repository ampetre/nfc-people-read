# Support — RoB NFC tag reader

## Before requesting help

Please include:

- app version;
- Android version and phone model;
- whether NFC is enabled;
- the exact error message or a screenshot;
- whether the issue occurs while reading, reviewing, or exporting.

## Public support channel

Open an issue in the public repository:

<https://github.com/ampetre/nfc-people-read/issues>

Do not include real participant names, exported attendance files, NFC identifiers, passwords, or other personal information in a public issue.
